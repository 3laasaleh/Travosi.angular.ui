02 — Section One

Travel Web Application — EGP 150,000

A customer-facing travel website that presents the agency's catalogue clearly, helps visitors discover relevant experiences, and guides qualified travellers into booking requests.

Home & navigation

Hero content, featured destinations, tours, packages and blogs; responsive navigation, mega menus, language and currency controls.

Destinations & cities

Destination and city listing/detail pages, image galleries, contextual city discovery, related tours and human-readable URLs.

Tours & Nile cruises

Searchable listings with filters, price/discount presentation, tour detail galleries, itinerary, availability context and booking entry point.

Holiday packages

Package discovery, date and search filtering, detail pages, itineraries, inclusions, related tours, price and booking journey.

Blogs & travel content

Article index and detail pages with bilingual content sections, imagery and SEO-ready page structure.

Accounts & reviews

Registration, sign-in, profile and booking history; completed-trip review workflow to support trustworthy customer feedback.

Booking experience

Traveller counts, dates, special requests, active discounts, price calculation and a booking request hand-off to the CRM team.

SEO & performance foundation

Server-rendered public routes, metadata, canonical URLs, sitemap-ready structure, bilingual routing and responsive layouts.

Web application deliverables

  • Responsive experience for laptop, tablet and mobile.
  • English/Arabic content presentation with right-to-left Arabic layout.
  • Shared catalogue data for destinations, cities, tours, packages, blogs and reviews.
  • Connected booking flow that creates actionable requests for the operations team.

04 — Section Two

CRM & Back Office — EGP 100,000

A role-protected internal system for the agency team. It centralises operational work from customer record and quotation through booking, invoice, voucher and follow-up.

Customer record→Travel quotation→Booking management→Invoice→Voucher

Customers & travellers

Customer profiles, company/personal details, contact information and companion travellers for reusable trip planning.

Quotations

Build, edit, duplicate, send and export branded PDF quotations using available tours, packages, hotels, flights and transfer lines.

Bookings desk

Central booking queue with status workflow, cancellation handling, confirmation notes and agent assignment.

Invoices & vouchers

Create financial invoices and operational vouchers with downloadable branded PDF documents for customer and supplier communication.

Catalogue management

Manage destinations, cities, tours, packages, blogs, hotels, airlines, flights and countries from the secure back office.

Tasks & notifications

Operational tasks, follow-up reminders and notification visibility for agents and administrators.

Pricing & promotions

Currency-aware product pricing, capacity controls and date-bound discount campaigns for tours and packages.

Inbox & control

Contact-message handling, publication status controls and authenticated access for administrative and agent workflows.
