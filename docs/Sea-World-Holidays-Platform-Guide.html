Document purpose

What this platform delivers

Sea World Holidays is a bilingual travel-commerce and operations platform. It gives customers one place to discover destinations, cities, tours and travel packages; check real availability; book as a registered customer or guest; track bookings; and share verified travel reviews. Behind the public experience, administrators and agents manage the content, inventory, customers, work assignments, bookings, quotations, invoices and service vouchers that keep the travel operation moving.

Core business value: the platform connects attractive destination content with a practical booking journey and an internal operations workspace. Customers receive clarity and convenience; the agency gains control, traceability, reusable data and a scalable foundation for future online payments.

Customer value

Easy discovery, clear itineraries and inclusions, localized content, currency-aware pricing, availability checks, flexible sign-in or guest booking, booking history and reviews.

Sales value

Promoted tours and packages, discount campaigns, related-tour recommendations, direct contact channels, lead capture, quotations and polished PDF documents.

Operations value

Central management of travel products, customers, tasks, bookings, flights, hotels, quotations, invoices, vouchers and customer questions.

Scope note: this guide distinguishes implemented transactional features from presentation-only pages. A live payment-provider checkout is not currently wired into the reviewed application. It can be added on demand as a controlled integration phase; see “Payment gateway readiness.”

Customer experience

The customer journey, step by step

Step 1Arrive and choose language
Step 2Search or browse inspiration
Step 3Compare tours and packages
Step 4Review full trip details
Step 5Select dates and travelers
Step 6Check live availability
Step 7Sign in or book as guest
Step 8Receive booking confirmation
Step 9Track booking status
Step 10Review the completed trip

1. Arrive in the right language and currency

Every public route is language-prefixed using /en/... or /ar/.... Selecting Arabic changes the content and the complete layout direction to RTL. The currency menu lets a visitor view supported prices in USD or EGP, using centrally managed exchange-rate logic.

2. Discover travel options

The visitor can use the global search box or browse destination, city, tour, Nile cruise and package menus. Home-page sections surface featured inventory and travel stories. Search results are grouped into destinations, tours and packages and lead directly to the relevant page.

3. Narrow the choice

Tour and package lists support date-based filtering, filter application and reset, page-size choice and pagination. Cards expose names, imagery, summaries, prices and active discounts. Destination and city pages provide geographic context and show relevant tours.

4. Build confidence on the detail page

Customers see a photo gallery, overview, duration, group/capacity information, price, itinerary timeline, highlights, included and excluded services, cancellation information where available, traveler reviews and related choices.

5. Configure the booking

The booking card collects the travel date or date range plus adult and child counts. It displays available dates, seat limits, price per adult/child, discounts and the calculated total before submission.

6. Confirm availability before commitment

Check Availability validates dates, party size and inventory. The customer receives an available/unavailable state and an available-seat count where applicable. Any booking-field change resets the previous availability result so stale availability is not reused.

7. Choose the identity path

A registered customer selects Sign In. The platform remembers the exact tour or package URL and returns to it after successful login. A visitor can alternatively select Book as a Guest and provide first name, last name, email and mobile number.

8. Create the booking

Book Now or Submit Guest Booking sends the validated product, dates and traveler counts. Loading, success and error feedback prevent duplicate submissions and make the result clear.

9. Track service progress

Signed-in customers open My Bookings to see booking reference, product, date, travelers, amount, status and a link back to the booked tour/package. Operations staff can assign an agent and progress the booking through confirmation, completion or cancellation.

10. Complete the feedback loop

After an eligible booking is completed, the customer can select a 1-5 rating, write a review and submit it. Approved product reviews appear on tour and package detail pages, strengthening trust for future customers.

Trust after travel

Bookings and verified reviews

1

Eligibility

The system checks that the booking matches the product, is completed and is eligible for review.

2

Customer input

The traveler selects 1-5 stars and writes a required comment up to the configured length.

3

Trust signal

Saved reviews contribute to the product’s aggregate rating and visible traveler feedback.

Customers can review from My Bookings and, when eligible, directly on the product detail page. Submission controls prevent incomplete ratings or empty text, provide a saving state, and remove the form after a successful review so the same booking is not reviewed repeatedly.

Agency workspace

Administration and role-based access

Configuration is the operating center behind the customer website. It is protected by authentication and an Admin/Agent role check. The workspace uses localized navigation and responsive table/grid views, making the same records manageable from different screen sizes.

Administrator

Broad management across catalogue content, customers, assignments, bookings, supporting inventory, commercial documents and customer messages.

Agent

Operational access according to the API’s authorization, including assigned work and task notifications. Customer assignment is automatic for agent-created customers.

Customer

No Configuration access. Customers use public pages, their booking history, settings and eligible review tools.

Agents receive task notifications and can mark one or all notifications as read. Signing out from any Configuration page clears the session and redirects to the localized home page, ensuring protected operational screens are not left displayed.

Optional expansion

Payment gateway readiness - available on demand

The platform is structurally ready for an online payment phase because it already knows the customer, product, travel dates, booking total, currency, quotation, invoice and booking status. A gateway can therefore be introduced without redesigning the discovery and booking experience.

What exists today

Booking amountsUSD / EGP displayQuotationsInvoicesVouchersPayment brand presentationPayment collection tasks

The public footer communicates accepted methods such as Mastercard, Visa, PayPal, InstaPay and Cash. The account area contains a payment-method UI. These elements do not, by themselves, constitute a secure live gateway.

What an on-demand gateway phase would add

  1. Provider selection: choose the gateway based on Egypt/international coverage, supported currencies, fees, settlement, refunds, recurring/tokenized cards and 3-D Secure requirements.
  2. Secure checkout: create a server-side payment session from a validated booking or invoice and redirect/embed the provider’s PCI-compliant payment experience.
  3. Payment states: Pending, Authorized/Paid, Failed, Cancelled, Refunded and Partially Refunded, linked to booking and invoice records.
  4. Webhook verification: accept signed provider callbacks so payment status is reliable even if the customer closes the browser.
  5. Receipts and notifications: show confirmation, update My Bookings, produce receipts and notify the customer/assigned agent.
  6. Admin controls: filter payments, inspect transaction IDs, reconcile totals, initiate allowed refunds and export records.
  7. Failure recovery: retry payment without duplicating the booking and preserve inventory according to an agreed reservation window.
  8. Security and compliance: never store raw card numbers or CVV; rely on provider tokenization, least-privilege secrets, audit logging and environment-specific configuration.
